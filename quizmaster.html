How scoring works

  • Correct = 1 point. Partly = ½ point (key idea right but the "why" is missing). Wrong = 0.
  • With 2 contestants, turns alternate. If steals are on and someone misses or only partly answers, the other contestant can steal for 1 point.
  • Host tip: the key ideas under the answer are what a full-credit answer should mention. Award "Correct" only if they explain why.
  • Keyboard: C correct · P partly · W wrong · N next · H hide/show answer · T pause timer.
